
# Side-Channel Attacks on Post-Quantum Cryptography Implementations

## Overview

This research survey examines side-channel vulnerabilities in post-quantum cryptographic implementations, focusing on attack techniques, countermeasures, and open research challenges.

## Research Scope

The survey covers:

- ML-KEM (FIPS 203)
- ML-DSA (FIPS 204)
- Falcon / FN-DSA
- SLH-DSA (FIPS 205)
- Power analysis
- Timing-based attacks
- Floating-point side-channel leakage
- Fault injection
- Implementation countermeasures

## Research Methodology

The work involved reviewing and analysing research literature on post-quantum cryptographic implementation security. The review considered attack methods, defensive techniques, implementation overheads, and remaining research challenges.
